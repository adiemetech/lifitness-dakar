## 20 minutes that count double

EMS — electrical muscle stimulation — sends light electrical impulses to your muscles while you perform exercises. Result: the muscle contracts harder and deeper than with willpower alone.

A session lasts 20 minutes. Not two hours. That is exactly why busy executives, entrepreneurs and parents are adopting it in Dakar: it fits between two meetings.

## How it actually works

1. You put on a vest with electrodes positioned on the major muscle groups: arms, chest, abs, glutes, thighs, back.
2. The coach sets the intensity zone by zone, based on your level and goals.
3. You chain guided exercises — squats, lunges, planks — while the impulses amplify every contraction.
4. End of session: you have worked the entire body, in depth, without heavy loads on the joints.

## What EMS really changes

- **Superior muscle recruitment.** Engaging deep fibers is hard to achieve with classic weight training, especially for beginners.
- **Less joint stress.** No heavy bar on your back. The effort is muscular, not mechanical.
- **Real time savings.** 20 minutes of EMS mobilize the whole body when a full gym session takes 60 to 90 minutes.
- **A mandatory framework.** Every movement is supervised by a coach: technique is corrected continuously.

## Who is it for? Who is it not for?

EMS suits most profiles: beginners who want a strong start, experienced athletes as a complement, busy people, supervised return-to-sport.

However, it is **not recommended** in case of: pregnancy, pacemaker, epilepsy, recent hernia or heart condition. When in doubt, get medical advice before your first session — we will ask you anyway.

## How often?

- **Getting started: 1 session per week.** The body is discovering; let it recover.
- **Cruising pace: 2 sessions per week maximum**, at least 48 hours apart.
- Ideal complement: one or two classic strength or cardio sessions alongside. EMS amplifies your training, it does not fully replace it.

## What results to expect, and when?

- **After 2 to 3 sessions:** you sleep better, you feel your muscles working differently, soreness proves the deep recruitment.
- **After 6 to 8 weeks** (at 2 sessions per week): visible tone, improved posture, a better sense of strength.
- **After 12 weeks:** measurable transformation if nutrition follows. No tool replaces a consistent plate.

Let us be clear: EMS is not magic. It is an amplifier. It multiplies your efforts, it does not replace them.

Devices sold online promise abs in front of the TV. The reality: without a coach to set intensities, correct positions and progress the workload, you stagnate — or you get injured.
